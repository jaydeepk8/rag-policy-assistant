Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 7 days of receiving the product.

Refunds are applicable only under the following conditions:
- The product is damaged at the time of delivery
- The product is defective or malfunctioning
- An incorrect product was delivered

Refund requests will not be accepted if:
- The product has been used or altered
- The request is made after the 7-day refund window
- The issue is due to customer mishandling

Once a refund request is approved, the refund will be processed within 5 to 7 business days.
The refund amount will be credited to the original payment method used during purchase.
